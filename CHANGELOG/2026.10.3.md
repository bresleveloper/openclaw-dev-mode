## 2026.10.3

### Highlights

- Updates that were stuck behind lost leases, completed-but-unrecognized receipts, or legacy 2026.9.8 activation records now recover and finish instead of refusing every retry. (#168388, #169038, #169027, #170195, #170170) Thanks @k94118, @mkdelta221, @waynegault, @chedong, and @Warhangel.
- Windows, macOS, and pnpm updates no longer leave the Gateway stopped or roll back: machine-qualified task accounts, short-name install paths, Doctor ancestry re-checks, protected Bun service commands, and pnpm 12 launchers all keep working. (#169481, #169292, #169563, #170006, #170003, #170131) Thanks @RomneyDa, @jokerman1302-eng, @xilopaint, and @k-sekula.
- Doctor no longer destroys data during repair: unreadable legacy auth keeps OAuth profiles, malformed UTF-8 memory files stay byte-exact, and blocked plugins keep their enabled state. (#170256, #169029, #168111) Thanks @Yolo521-bot, @wangmiao0668000666, and @obviyus.
- The "On this Mac" first run on a fresh Mac authenticates the app-hosted Gateway instead of failing with "Gateway rejected auth", and the Mac app reconnects after a trusted certificate renewal. (#169403, #168151)
- Chat-requested updates and plugin upgrades no longer roll back during Doctor cleanup, and stale Codex runtimes no longer break turns after an update. (#170218, #170306, #170339) Thanks @RomneyDa, @hannesrudolph, and @ezimerman.
- Agent turns are more reliable: the next turn after native compaction, ChatGPT WebSocket closes mid-stream, switching back to OpenAI without the Codex plugin, and Codex follow-ups after failed thread preparation all succeed. (#169583, #167512, #169412, #169431) Thanks @obviyus, @ericcurtin, @mightyx, @jjjhenriksen, and @RomneyDa.
